# Start Here

**Photo Digitizing Guide** — a plain-English manual for rescuing your family's printed photos, slides, and film negatives into a safe digital archive.

If you have a shoebox of old prints, a carousel of slides, or strips of negatives in an envelope marked "Christmas 1987," this guide is for you. No cloud accounts, no subscriptions, no special expertise required — just a phone, a flatbed scanner (if you have one), and a weekend or two.

## What this guide covers

1. [Phone vs. Flatbed: Choosing Your Capture Tool](01-phone-vs-flatbed.md) — which tool for which job, and when it doesn't matter.
2. [Lighting Setups](02-lighting-setups.md) — cheap, repeatable lighting that makes phone scans look professional.
3. [Resolution Honesty: 600 dpi vs. 300 dpi](03-resolution-honesty.md) — what those numbers actually buy you, and when "good enough" really is.
4. [File Naming: Years and Events](04-file-naming.md) — names your grandchildren will understand.
5. [Folder Trees](05-folder-trees.md) — a simple structure that scales to thousands of photos.
6. [Backup to Two Places](06-backup-two-places.md) — the 3-2-1 rule, adapted for real households.
7. [Sharing with Relatives](07-sharing-with-relatives.md) — getting copies to siblings and cousins without the cloud.
8. [Printing a Keepsake](08-printing-a-keepsake.md) — turning the archive back into something you can hold.
9. [Scanner Worksheets](09-scanner-worksheets.md) — printable checklists and batch logs.
10. [Glossary](10-glossary.md) — every term used in this guide, defined once.

## How to use this guide

- **Have one weekend and a phone?** Read chapters 1–3, then 4–6. You'll have a working archive.
- **Have a flatbed scanner?** Chapters 1 and 3 tell you when it earns its desk space.
- **Have a relative waiting for photos?** Skim 4–5, then jump to 7 (sharing) and 8 (keepsakes).

## The one rule that matters most

**Every photo should exist in at least two physical places before you call it "saved."** A scan on your laptop is not an archive — it's a single copy of something irreplaceable. Chapter 6 makes this painless.
